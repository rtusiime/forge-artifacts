Self-Rebalancing Lesson Calendar

Drag any lesson to a new day. Everything downstream re-solves to keep the rules true, moving as little as possible. The thing you sketched on July 13, running on your actual constraints. Proof of concept.

The rules it keeps

  • Prerequisites first. Within a challenge, lesson N must sit on an earlier day than lesson N+1.
  • At most 2 lessons a day across all challenges.
  • No two of the same subject on the same day (so one Math max per day).
  • Blocked days (field trips, no-school) hold nothing.
  • Minimal disruption. Keep every lesson near where it was; move only what the rules force.

Solver: a greedy list-scheduler (topological order, place at first feasible day at or after where you want it). Instant for this size. A production version would swap in an optimizing solver (OR-Tools CP-SAT or Timefold) for provably minimal moves. No backend, no subscription: this whole tool is one HTML file.
