Feasibility, counted your way (math Rosenshine blocks, not self-reported challenge weeks): each band needs ~6–16 math blocks across its run — roughly one math block every 3–5 days at 1 block/day. Math volume is not the binding constraint. The real constraint is ordering: the daily Eureka sequence must deliver a module's instruction before the challenge applies it (e.g. G6 M1 unit rate must land before St. Hubert's adoption-rate block). Complementary blocks that can share a 2-block day: Gardens geometry + a ratio block; St. Hubert's stats + geometry.
