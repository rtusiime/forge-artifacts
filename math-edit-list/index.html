New-lesson specs (the 3 inserts)

① Uniforms 5/6 · new 1.B.3 — "Say it as a rate: votes per one" · 6.RP.A.2
Objective: Students express a vote comparison as a unit rate ("for every 1 vote for A, C got about 3") and use rate language, before converting to percent. Placement: immediately after 1.B.2 (ratio), before percent (renumbered 1.B.4). Context: the class-vote counts already on the board — no new data. Closes: break #1.
② Uniforms 5/6 · new 4.C.0 — "What makes a question statistical, and the shape of our data" · 6.SP.A.1, 6.SP.A.2
Objective: Students pose a statistical question about the survey and describe the response distribution by center, spread, and overall shape — before computing or displaying. Placement: start of Act 4, before 4.C.2 (totals) and 4.D.1 (charts). Closes: the 6.SP.A foundation hole under every 5/6 stats lesson.
③ Uniforms 7/8 · new Act 1 beat — "Rates and proportion on our own data" · 7.RP.A.2 (folds in 7.RP.A.1)
Objective: Students test whether two quantities on the vote/spreadsheet are proportional and compute a constant of proportionality, incl. rates with fractional quantities. Placement: Act 1, after spreadsheet basics, before weighted scoring (3.C.1). Closes: break #2.
